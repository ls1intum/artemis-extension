### What you can change

**Iris and your files**
`artemis.iris.sendUncommittedChanges` is on by default, which lets Iris read your
unsaved, unstaged and unpushed files so it can answer about the code you are
actually writing. Turn it off if you would rather Iris only saw what you pushed.

**Proactive help**
`artemis.struggleDetection.enabled` lets the extension notice when you seem stuck.
`artemis.struggleDetection.showInterventions` controls whether it says anything
when it does.

**Where the extension opens**
`artemis.startPage` decides what you land on after signing in: the course
dashboard, or the exercise detected in your current workspace.
